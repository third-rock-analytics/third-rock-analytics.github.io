Questions

Common questions

Anything else, just ask on the call.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After the free call you get a written scope with either a fixed price or an hour estimate, so you know the cost before any work starts. Nonprofit and grant-funded projects get a reduced rate.

Do you work with private landowners?

Yes. Alongside nonprofits and agencies, I work with farms, vineyards, ranches, and other private land managers.

Do you only work in Washington?

No. Most of the work is done remotely, so location isn't a limit. If you're in Washington, site visits and in-person presentations are also an option.

What data do I need to have?

Possibly none. Free public sources like satellite imagery, aerial photos, and elevation data cover a lot of questions. If you do have field data, spreadsheets, or GIS files, bring them and we'll put them to use.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope, and your written scope includes a timeline. If you have a hard deadline, mention it on the call. Rush timelines are possible.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's what the free call is for. Describe the question you're trying to answer, and I'll suggest what would actually help, even if it's not a project with me.
